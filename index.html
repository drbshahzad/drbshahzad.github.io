About Me

Dr. Basit Shahzad is an Associate Professor of Software Engineering at the University of Prince Mugrin, Saudi Arabia. He completed his Postdoctoral research at the University of Cambridge, U.K., and holds advanced degrees in software engineering and computing from Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS (UTP), Malaysia, and the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST), Pakistan. Previously, he held academic roles at King Saud University, NUML, and the COMSATS Institute of Information Technology.

What truly sets Dr. Shahzad apart is his forward-looking research agenda, which combines technical rigor with a profound commitment to social impact. Through vibrant international collaborations, he tackles high-stakes domains—pioneering intelligent agentic software engineering, designing resilient systems for smart cities, championing green computing for environmental sustainability, and driving innovations in computing for social good.
