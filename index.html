About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at University Name, directing the Intelligent Systems & Edge Computing Lab. My core research focuses on bridging the gap between heavy AI models and resource-constrained edge systems, emphasizing efficiency, model compression, and safe human-AI collaboration.
