About Me

Dr. Basit Shahzad is an Associate Professor of Software Engineering at the University of Prince Mugrin, Saudi Arabia. He received his M.Sc. degree from the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST), Islamabad, Pakistan, and his Ph.D. degree in Software Engineering from Universiti Teknologi PETRONAS (UTP), Malaysia. Furthermore, he completed his Postdoctoral research at the Computer Laboratory, University of Cambridge, U.K., where he currently continues as a Visiting Scientist.

Dr. Shahzad has previously held academic and research positions at the COMSATS Institute of Information Technology (Islamabad), King Saud University (Riyadh), and National University of Modern Languages (NUML). His main research agenda focuses on high-impact software engineering challenges, software risk management, blockchain-based decentralized solutions, social media analytics, and empirical software development cost estimation.
